An RTOS-grade network — on a range.

Wireless mesh,
real-time firmware,
tablet command.
Step 1

Target raises into position.

A DC motor drives the target upward through four optical position sensors. The MCU tracks each segment in real time and brakes at the top via a two-stage PWM profile.

Motor · Position sensing
Step 2

Hit sensor arms and waits.

Zephyr RTOS on the nRF52840 holds off the magnetic hit sensor for 500 ms after reaching top — a debounce window tuned to reject EMI from the RF front-end at motor stop. Then polling begins at 50 ms.

Firmware · Sensor debounce
Step 3

Hit hops through the mesh.

A BLE Mesh notification propagates relay-to-relay to the provisioner at TTL 10. Redundant paths, no infrastructure required.

RF Fabric
Step 4

Tablet scores & reacts.

Score updates on the Range Console. Scripted drills cue the next engagement — target drops, LEDs flash, next node arms.

UI · End-to-end
